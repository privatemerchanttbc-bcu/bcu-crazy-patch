================================================================================
              ADVENTURE MODE - The Totally Official Survival Manual
================================================================================

Welcome, brave Cat Commander. You have played Battle Cats the normal way for
years: deploy units, watch them shuffle right, sip your coffee, win. Adorable.

Adventure Mode looked at that and said "no". Here, you control exactly ONE cat,
with your own two hands, in real time, while an angry parade of enemies tries to
turn you into a fine paste. There is no cannon. There is no worker cat. There is
no retreat. There is only you, a keyboard, and poor life decisions.

Let's get you killed as slowly as possible.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CONTROLS  (a.k.a. Keyboard Twister)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   A / D  ....  Move left / right. Yes, it's WASD but the cat forgot W and S.
   J      ....  Attack. Mash responsibly. Real attack, real hitbox.
   K      ....  Jump. Also a dodge: you're briefly INVINCIBLE mid-air.
   L      ....  Conjure. Summon an ally IF your unit can. ~5s cooldown.
   W      ....  Teleport. Only if you grabbed the TELEPORT CORE. Blink like you
                mean it.
   P      ....  Pause menu. Freezes the chaos. Resume / Restart / Save & Quit.

That's it. Five fingers, infinite regret. If your unit has no summon, L does
nothing and judges you silently.

Fun fact: hit detection is pixel-perfect (image-boundary combat is force-locked
ON, so don't bother with F7 - we already flipped that switch for you). If your
sword visibly touches an enemy, it connects. If it whiffs, that was skill issue.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GETTING STARTED  (Choose Your Fighter)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. On the main menu, click "Adventure Mode". A shiny 3-slot save screen opens.
  2. Each slot is either Empty (New Game) or shows your saved run: the unit, the
     stage, lives left, cores collected, and when you last rage-quit.
  3. Pick an empty slot -> New Game -> the unit picker opens.

  THE UNIT PICKER
  Every player unit from every pack you own is here. All of them. Even the
  embarrassing ones. There's a live animation preview so you can admire your cat
  before it dies.
    - Type in the search box to filter by name or pack.
    - The "Conjure" column shows a checkmark if the unit can summon with L.
    - Set your Lives (1-9, default 3). More lives = more chances to embarrass
      yourself.

  CASUAL vs IRONMAN  (choose your pain level)
    - CASUAL : die and you keep your checkpoint. Reload, retry the stage,
      pretend it never happened. The gaming equivalent of "undo".
    - IRONMAN: die and your save slot is DELETED. Gone. Vaporized. Your run,
      your cores, your dreams - all of it. Ironman slots wear a little skull
      badge so you remember the stakes every time you look at them. Brave.
      Foolish. We respect it.

  Pick your unit, hit OK, and off you go.

  "CONTINUE LAST RUN"
  Too impatient for the slot screen? The 3-slot dialog has a "Continue Last Run"
  button at the top that jumps straight back into your most recent save. For
  people who fear commitment to menus.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE GAMEPLAY LOOP  (Fight, Sweat, Repeat)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each stage is a chunk of the Empire of Cats campaign, reimagined as a brawler:

  1. Enemies pour in from the left. Up to 8 at a time, because we love you.
  2. You beat them up. They beat you up. It's a whole thing.
  3. Clear every enemy and a PORTAL DOOR appears. It looks suspiciously like a
     Minecraft Nether gate because our art department has *taste*.
  4. Walk into the door -> flashy transition -> POWER CORE selection -> next
     stage.

Do this until you either win the campaign or die trying. Spoiler: it's usually
the second one.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. POWER CORES  (The Good Stuff)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Between stages you get offered 3 cores. Pick ONE:
    - A / D to move the cursor, J to confirm.
    - Cores STACK for the entire run. Grab 20 damage cores and become a war
      crime.
    - The tier climbs as you go: Bronze -> Silver -> Gold -> Platinum -> Legend.
      Legend cores are the ones that make the game beg for mercy.

There are dozens of cores. A tasting menu:

    Might / Crit / Berserker  - hit harder, hit crit-ier, hit angrier.
    Vitality / Guard / Regen / Lifesteal - live longer, spite the healers.
    Swiftness / Haste / Frenzy - zoom and swing like a caffeinated blender.
    Sky Leap / Featherfall     - jump to the moon, fall like a leaf.
    Wave Core   - your hits launch a piercing wave that shoves enemies back.
    Surge Core  - hits erupt in a molten zone. Very warm. Enemies dislike it.
    Teleport Core - unlocks the W blink. Now you're basically a magician.

And the spicy UNIQUE cores, from "neat" to "why is this legal":

    Titan Core   - become HUGE. Double size, double HP, extra damage. Personal
                   space? No.
    Glass Cannon - triple damage, 40% HP. Live fast, die embarrassingly.
    One Punch    - every hit has a chance to just... delete an enemy. No notes.
    Afterimage   - your hits leave blue ghost clones that repeat the attack.
    Golden God   - upgrades EVERY core you own by a tier. Greed, rewarded.
    Second Wind  - cheat death once per stage. A free continue from the universe.

Read the cards. Or don't. Chaos is a valid strategy.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DYING, LIVES, AND THE PAUSE BUTTON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You have lives. When your HP hits zero:
    - Lives remaining? You respawn. Shake it off, champ.
    - Out of lives?    Game Over. Casual keeps your checkpoint; Ironman feeds
      your save to the void (see above, and maybe shed a single tear).

  THE PAUSE MENU (P)
  Press P to freeze time and open the menu. Navigate with W/S (or A/D), confirm
  with J:
    - Resume         - back to the violence.
    - Restart Stage  - redo the current stage from the top. For when it all went
      wrong.
    - Save & Quit to Menu - bail out cleanly and come back later. Progress waits.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE SAVE SYSTEM  (a.k.a. "It Actually Remembers You")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - AUTOSAVE: at the start of every stage, your run is quietly saved - unit,
      stage, lives, and all your precious cores. No button needed. We got you.
    - Saves live in their own folders on disk, each with a backup copy, because
      we assume the worst about hard drives.
    - Turn off the game mid-run, come back next week, hit Continue - you're right
      where you left off (at the start of that stage; we save checkpoints, not
      your exact mid-air flailing).

  Note: checkpoints are per-stage. Reloading drops you at the stage's beginning,
  not the precise pixel where you panicked. Consider it a mulligan.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ZOMBIES AND OTHER PARTY CRASHERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Some enemies are ZOMBIES. You kill them, they lie down for a dramatic nap, then
pop back up for round two. In Adventure Mode we keep the nap SHORT (about a
second) so the action stays snappy - no more staring at a corpse wondering if the
game froze.

And no, you cannot skip their revive by cannonballing onto the body anymore. We
patched your cheese. The zombies are invincible while napping. Let them have this.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. PRO TIPS FROM CATS WHO DIED SO YOU DON'T HAVE TO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - JUMP IS A DODGE. Big scary attack incoming? K over it. Invincibility frames
      are your best friend and your only friend.
    - FIGHT ON BOTH SIDES. You attack whichever way you face. Weave through the
      crowd, don't just stand there like a deploy-and-pray cat.
    - SNOWBALL YOUR CORES. Stacking one theme (all damage, or all speed) usually
      beats a scattered mess. Commit to the bit.
    - HEALTHY = POWERFUL. Cores like Battle Trance and Slipstream only work while
      your HP is high. Don't get slapped around.
    - IRONMAN IS FOR CLOSERS. Try Casual first. Ironman is a flex, not a start.
    - WAVE AND SURGE are crowd control gold. Facing a mob? Point at it and swing.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. TROUBLESHOOTING  (When Cats Attack Your Expectations)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Q: My cat won't summon anything with L!
  A: Your unit doesn't have a summon ability. The Conjure column would've had a
     checkmark. This is a "read the label" situation.

  Q: W does nothing!
  A: You need the TELEPORT CORE. Until then, W is just a lonely key having an
     existential crisis.

  Q: An enemy is standing still doing nothing!
  A: It's probably a zombie taking its brief revive nap, or it's out of range
     plotting. Give it a second. If a LIVING enemy truly freezes forever, that's
     a bug - tell the dev and mention it happened after a jump.

  Q: I died and my whole save vanished!
  A: You picked IRONMAN, you magnificent daredevil. That's the deal. The skull
     badge tried to warn you.

  Q: This is really hard.
  A: Yes. That's the feature. Adventure Mode is not here to be your friend. It's
     here to be your worthy adversary. Now get back in there.
